Example review: ExifTool 12.76

ℹ️ This page shows the format of OSINTario reviews, the same one we use for sponsored tools. ExifTool is not a sponsor: we chose it because it can be fully tested offline and anyone can repeat the tests with our sample file.

Review card

Tool
ExifTool 12.76 (Phil Harvey), package libimage-exiftool-perl 12.76+dfsg-1
What it is for
Reading, exporting and removing metadata from photos, documents and videos.
Date
25 November 2026
Environment
Isolated Linux container (Ubuntu 24.04.5 LTS, 2 cores), no network (every test ran under unshare -n).
Test data
7 files created by OSINTario with fictitious data: JPG photo with GPS (coordinates of the Obelisco in Buenos Aires) and thumbnail, the same photo re-saved, a PNG renamed to .jpg, a PDF, a DOCX and an MP4 video with location.
Installation
sudo apt install libimage-exiftool-perl: 3.8 s, 23.6 MB.
Cost and licence
Free. Perl licence (Artistic or GPL).
Sponsorship
No. This is an example of the format.

Verdict

4,6 / 5

Recommended. It correctly read everything we gave it in all 5 formats and did not modify any original. It is essential for metadata, with three precautions: work on copies, open suspicious files inside a VM, and treat metadata as a lead, not as proof (we forged them ourselves in seconds to build this review).

What we evaluate

Each criterion goes from 1 to 5. The score comes from the tests below, not from what the vendor says.

  • Accuracy●●●●● 510 out of 10 tests returned the exact value we had written.
  • Format coverage●●●●● 5JPG, PNG, PDF, DOCX and MP4 without add-ons.
  • Forensic value●●●●○ 4Reading does not change the file (same SHA-256 before and after). It loses one point because the removal option overwrites without asking for confirmation.
  • Installation●●●●○ 4One line and under 4 seconds. The Ubuntu 24.04 version lags behind the official one.
  • Ease of use●●●○○ 3Command line only, with many tag names. In the video, the readable location only appeared when asking for all groups.
  • Privacy●●●●● 5It worked fully without network: your files never leave your computer.
  • Performance●●●●● 5500 photos to JSON in 1.5 to 1.6 s (3 runs, 2 cores).
  • Cost, licence and maintenance●●●●● 5Free, open source and maintained by its author since 2003.

Tests: what we did, what came out and why

#What we testedHowResultWhy
1JPG photo with GPS, camera and dateexiftool -n -GPSLatitude -GPSLongitude -Make -Model -DateTimeOriginal -OffsetTimeOriginal foto-gps.jpg✅ Coordinates -34.603722, -58.381592, camera, author, software, date and time zone -03:00, exact.It reads the standard EXIF and GPS blocks. With -n it gives numbers ready to paste into a map.
2Embedded thumbnailexiftool -b -ThumbnailImage foto-gps.jpg > miniatura.jpg✅ It extracted a thumbnail showing the “ORIGINAL SIN EDITAR” (unedited) version, byte-for-byte identical to the one we stored.Some editors change the image but not the thumbnail. It is a classic sign that a photo was edited.
3PDFexiftool -Author -Title -Creator -Producer -CreateDate informe.pdf✅ Author, title, creator software, producer and date.It reads the PDF information dictionary, where the creator's name is often left.
4Word document (DOCX)exiftool -Creator -LastModifiedBy -Description -CreateDate memo.docx✅ Author “Juan Ejemplo”, last edited by “Maria Revisora”, comment “Version 3 - borrador” and creation date.It reads the document's internal properties. Who modified it last is often a valuable lead.
5MP4 video with locationexiftool -a -G1 video.mp4⚠️ Correct recording date and coordinates. With the short command, the location came out as raw text (-34.6037-058.3816/).It works, but to see the decoded location you must ask for all tags with -a -G1.
6Fake extension (PNG renamed to .jpg)exiftool -validate -warning -FileType disfrazado.jpg✅ It detected a PNG and warned: “File has wrong extension”.It identifies the type by the file's internal signature, not by its name.
7Re-saved photo (as when uploaded to social media)exiftool -GPSLatitude -Make -DateTimeOriginal foto-reguardada.jpg✅ No GPS, no camera, no date: it returned nothing.That is the correct result: the data no longer exists. It is not a failure, but it teaches something key: an empty result proves nothing.
8Removing metadata from a copyexiftool -all= -overwrite_original copia.jpg✅ No GPS, author or thumbnail remained. The original kept its SHA-256 because we worked on a copy.Useful to protect your own photos before publishing them. Never on original evidence: it overwrites without asking.
9Batch of 500 photos to JSONexiftool -json -r -n -GPSLatitude -GPSLongitude -DateTimeOriginal carpeta/✅ 500 correct records in 1.5 to 1.6 s.It processes whole folders and the JSON output can be loaded into a spreadsheet or another program.
10Integrity: reading does not modifysha256sum foto-gps.jpg informe.pdf memo.docx video.mp4✅ Same SHA-256 before and after every read.It can be used on evidence without breaking the chain of custody, as long as you only read.

SWOT

Strengths

  • Full accuracy in all 10 tests.
  • Reads photos, documents and videos with a single tool.
  • Does not touch the original when reading and works offline.
  • Exports to JSON and CSV for batch work.

Opportunities

  • Combine it with Multi-OSINT Lite: first a quick look in the browser, then ExifTool for the report.
  • Always check the thumbnail and the real extension: two leads few people look at.
  • Use it to clean your own photos before publishing them.

Weaknesses

  • Command line only, with very long output.
  • The Ubuntu 24.04 version (12.76) lags behind the official one.
  • It cannot recover metadata that was already removed.

Threats

  • Social media and messaging apps strip metadata on upload: often there will be nothing to read.
  • Metadata is easy to forge: we wrote the ones for this test with the same tool.
  • Reading malicious files had serious flaws in the past (CVE-2021-22204, fixed in 12.24): keep it updated and open suspicious files in a VM.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Accurate and fast.
  • Free and open source.
  • Works offline.
  • Finds hidden leads, such as the original thumbnail.

👎 Cons

  • Learning curve: many commands and tags.
  • Removal overwrites without confirmation.
  • No official graphical interface on Linux.

How to repeat the tests

  1. Download our sample file and check its SHA-256 fingerprint.
  2. Run tests 1 and 2 on that file.
  3. Compare your results with the table.
