02 · Directory

Tool directory

Each card shows its status and whether it needs a login or payment. Tools marked "use with caution" may be legal where you live and still require authorization, a clear purpose or a separate research account.

⚖️ Ethical and lawful use. OSINTario is a guide to open-source investigation, not a permission slip. Every search about a person needs a legitimate purpose and a legal basis (GDPR and local laws). Do not use it to harass, monitor partners or exes, expose personal data or access systems without authorisation. Whoever searches is responsible for what they do with the information.

Login: you need a (free) account to use it. Paid: the tool or its useful features cost money. Never investigate with your personal account. 🧪 The tool must be installed: follow the safe installation guide.

03 · Method

The investigation cycle

Tools change every month. Method is what makes a report survive someone else's review.

  1. Scope and legal basisWho asks, what for and with what authorization. No lawful purpose, no investigation.
  2. DiscoverDomains, IPs, subdomains, accounts and exposed services.
  3. EnrichDNS, metadata, breaches, relationships between entities.
  4. ThreatsIOCs, phishing, malicious infrastructure and actors.
  5. Digital footprintRepositories, social media, paste sites, Telegram channels.
  6. ValidateTwo independent sources per finding, with date and confidence level.
  7. ReportArchived evidence, a hash for every capture and proportionate conclusions.
BreachesAn email in a leak is a lead. Confirm it with another source.
UsernamesThe same handle on two networks is a hypothesis: namesakes are common.
PhonesDirectories age fast. Cross-check before you write anything down.
ImagesReverse search shows where it appeared, not where it was taken.
BlockchainFlows are public; identity needs off-chain evidence.
EvidenceURL, UTC date, capture, SHA-256 hash and archived copy.
